Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

StreamClipper is a personal tool used only by its operator, with the operator's own accounts.

What data is accessed

  • Basic profile information (account ID, username) of the operator's connected TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos to those accounts.
  • The operator's own public Twitch stream recordings.

How it is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer and are never shared with or sold to anyone. No data about other people is collected or stored. Videos are processed locally to remove personal information and profanity before publishing.

Third parties

Stream transcripts and on-screen text are sent to Anthropic's API solely to choose clips and detect personal information for removal. No data is used for advertising.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's connected-apps settings; local tokens are deleted on request.
